Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026.

Infinity Digital Development (“we,” “us”) builds AI-powered websites and related services for small businesses. Scott Smith operates the company from Denver, Colorado. This policy covers infinitydigitaldevelopment.com and business email we send from this domain. Our main website is infinitydigitaldev.com.

This website

This domain is a simple company site. It does not have accounts, a contact form, payments, or analytics. We do not set advertising or tracking cookies here. If you only read these pages, the information involved is limited to what your browser sends when it requests a page — typically an IP address, browser type, and the page you asked for — which the host of the site may keep in ordinary server logs.

Email we send

We use infinitydigitaldevelopment.com to send business-to-business email about our services, including AI-powered websites, AI chat, and automation. A message from an address on this domain is from Infinity Digital Development.

Those emails are for business contacts. They are not directed at children.

Information we use

For outreach and replies, we use business contact details such as:

  • Name
  • Work email address
  • Company name and role
  • A note about how we found the address, or what you told us if you replied

We get this from publicly available business sources, from people who contact us, and from the conversation if you write back or call. We do not ask you to create an account on this site.

How we use it

We use contact details to send relevant messages about our services, to answer you, and to do work you ask us to do. We do not sell personal information.

How to opt out

You can stop marketing email from us at any time:

  • Reply to the message and ask to unsubscribe, or use the unsubscribe link in the email.
  • Call 720-252-7037.
  • Write to us through the contact page at infinitydigitaldev.com/contact.

Tell us the email address you want removed. We will stop sending marketing mail to that address and honor the request promptly. A reply that is only about an active project you asked for is not marketing.

Who we share it with

We share contact details only with service providers that send email for us or host this website, and only so they can do that work, or when the law requires it. Those providers may not use the information for their own marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep outreach details until you opt out, ask us to delete them, or we no longer need them. If you become a client, we keep project records for as long as the work and ordinary business records require.

Your requests

You can ask what business contact information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Call us or use the contact page on the main site. We may need to confirm that the request comes from the person the address belongs to.

Children

Our site and emails are meant for businesses. They are not directed at anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
